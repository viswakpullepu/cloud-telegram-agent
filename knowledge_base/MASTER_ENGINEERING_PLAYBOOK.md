## 1. Typography & Aesthetic Pairing Masterclass
- **Display Headings**: `Syne` (Black 900 / ExtraBold 800) for monumental futuristic presence.
- **Body & Bento Titles**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean, modern readability.
- **Code, Telemetry & CLI**: `JetBrains Mono` for developer & cyber aesthetic.

## 2. Color Science & Contrast Harmonies
- **Void Space**: `#020408` to `#0A0E1A` (95%+ contrast ratio for neon elements).
- **Cyber Accents**: `#00F2FE` (Cyber Cyan), `#00FF87` (Spring Emerald), `#9D4EDD` (Hyper Violet), `#F59E0B` (Amber Gold).
- **Glass Surfaces**: `rgba(10, 14, 24, 0.78)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and subtle border highlights.

## 3. High-Performance WebGL Shaders & Physics
- **Shader Stability**: Always use `alpha: true` and `antialias: true` on WebGLRenderer.
- **Dynamic Pixel Ratio**: Cap `renderer.setPixelRatio(Math.min(window.devicePixelRatio, 2))` to prevent mobile GPU throttling.
- **Camera Aspect Resize**: Bind reactive resize listener to update `camera.aspect` and `camera.updateProjectionMatrix()`.
